DESK ARCHIVE
ABOUT/

一座连接桌面档案与现场体验的内容档案库，

以祭坛、餐桌、课桌、绘图桌与电脑桌五个桌面单元组织词条，沿时间线索与桌面类型学两条路径，追问一张约一平方米的平面如何承载仪式、知识、权力、技术与记忆。

展厅中的每一块 1㎡ 网格在这里拥有对应的词条，而每一个词条，都将在展厅中找到自己的位置。

An archive connecting tabletop stories with exhibition experiences, organized into five desk units –
Altar,
Dining Table, School Desk, Drawing Table and Computer Desk.
Along a timeline and a typology of desks, it asks how a surface of roughly one square meter carries ritual, knowledge, power, technology, and memory. Every 1㎡ grid module in the gallery has its corresponding entry here, and every entry will find its place in the gallery.
